Financial Identity

Who issues your financial documents. Every invoice, receipt and adjustment note draws its letterhead, tax details, payment instructions and sign-off from here — set it once rather than per document.

Legal entity

The entity that issues the document — often longer than the site name. Fills {{ '{business_legal_name}' }}; blank falls back to the site name.

Fills {{ '{business_number}' }}. An invoice for a taxable sale without an ABN can trigger 47% withholding by the payer.

Shown on documents for payment queries. Blank hides the line.

%

Australia is 10%. Prices are quoted GST-inclusive.

One line per row, printed under the ABN on the document. A “C/- Dr Jane Smith” care-of line can be the first row.

Payment details

Fills {{ '{payment_instructions}' }} — the EFT / BSB / account details printed on invoices. Receipts don’t show this.

You can use {{ '{sanitized_invoice_ref}' }} here for the payer to quote — the reference with the dashes taken out, which fits an 18-character bank reference field and survives banks that reject punctuation. It resolves to the invoice (INV2026080628D4) on a sponsor invoice and to the member's registration (REG000123) on a registration payment request, so one block of text works for both. Searching the ledger or registrations for that form finds what it belongs to.
